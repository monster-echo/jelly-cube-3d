Twist the jelly, line up the colors, and watch the whole cube wobble into place. Jelly Cube 3D turns the classic cube puzzle into something soft, bouncy, and oddly satisfying — every slice you turn squishes and springs back.

Each level hands you a scrambled jelly cube. Rotate slices to restore every face to a single color, in as few moves as you can. Early stages warm you up on a 3×3 cube; later levels stretch your brain across bigger cubes and pattern challenges like checkerboards and jelly donuts.

HOW TO PLAY
• Swipe to twist any slice of the jelly cube
• Solve every face in one color to clear the level
• Beat the move limit for 3 stars and bonus coins
• Use a hint when you're stuck, or undo freely to retrace your twists

FEATURES
• 30 hand-tuned story levels, from your first 3×3 squish to 4×4 master challenges
• Daily Challenge with a global seed — same puzzle for everyone, streak rewards included
• Free play on 3×3, 4×4, 5×5, and giant 9×9, 10×10 jelly cubes
• 10 collectible skins across jelly, AI art, and special-effect looks, plus 4 material styles (jelly, plush, liquid, sand)
• Global leaderboards for every challenge size
• Hints, undo, and a camera you can orbit to check the back faces
• Coin economy: solve to earn coins, watch a rewarded video to double your bonus or top up for a hint

PROGRESSION
Progress is saved on your device, so you can play anywhere. Levels cost nothing to retry, and there's no account to create.

The game is supported by ads: an occasional interstitial between levels, a banner on the home screen, and optional rewarded videos (only when you choose to double a reward or grab a hint). No in-app purchases, no subscription, no account system.
