# AppGallery — English Listing Draft

## App name
Jelly Cube 3D

## Short description
Twist, squish and solve a glossy 3D jelly cube.

## Full description
Jelly Cube 3D is a real-time, soft-body twisty puzzle with the feel of a dessert-table showpiece.

- Twist a layer and watch it overshoot, wobble and settle.
- Poke a jellylet and see the dent ripple through its neighbours.
- Drop the cube onto its ceramic plate and feel the squash and rebound.
- Spin the whole puzzle with two fingers; pinch to zoom.
- Scramble and solve 2×2, 3×3 and 4×4 cubes.
- Procedural audio and haptics make every snap and squish tactile.
- Offline gameplay, no ads, no analytics, no account.

The cube is mathematically strict underneath the jelly: integer-position cubelets, 120 Hz physics, and a state that returns exactly to solved.

## v1 HarmonyOS availability

2×2, 3×3 and 4×4 only. The 5×5/6×6 Extra Cube Types unlock is currently
available on iOS and Android; Huawei IAP Kit is planned for a later HarmonyOS
release. Do not advertise 5×5/6×6 in AppGallery until that is implemented.
