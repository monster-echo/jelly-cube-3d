## Full description
Jelly Cube 3D is a real-time, soft-body twisty puzzle with the feel of a dessert-table showpiece.

- **Twist** a layer and watch it overshoot, wobble, and settle into place.
- **Poke** a jellylet and see the dent ripple through its neighbours.
- **Drop** the cube onto its ceramic plate and feel the squash and rebound.
- **Spin** the whole puzzle with two fingers; pinch to zoom.
- **Scramble** and solve 2×2, 3×3 and 4×4 cubes. 5×5 and 6×6 are available as an optional one-time unlock.
- **Solve by hand** after a scramble for a small, gooey surprise.
- Procedural audio and haptics make every snap and squish tactile.
- Fully offline: no account, no ads, no analytics, no network required for gameplay.

The cube is mathematically strict underneath the jelly: integer-position cubelets, 120 Hz physics, and a state that returns exactly to solved. It is a puzzle you can fidget with and a puzzle you can actually solve.
