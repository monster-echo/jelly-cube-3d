The app has no account registration, login, or account deletion (no account system exists), and no user-generated content, so there are no content reporting or blocking mechanisms to demonstrate.

2) APP PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE
Jelly Cube 3D is a real-time soft-body twisty puzzle — a Rubik's-style cube rendered and simulated like glossy jelly. Every layer turn overshoots and wobbles, pokes dent the surface, and the cube squashes when dropped. It is both a fidget toy and a mathematically exact puzzle (integer cube state, exact return to solved). Target audience: general puzzle and casual-game players, age 4 and up. It provides a tactile, relaxing 3D puzzle that can actually be solved, fully offline, with no account and no ads.

3) HOW TO SET UP AND ACCESS ALL MAIN FEATURES
No login, no account, no sample files, no configuration required — launch and play.
• Free content: 2×2, 3×3 and 4×4 cubes are available immediately. Drag a layer to twist; use the tool capsule for Poke and Drop; two-finger drag to orbit and pinch to zoom; Scramble, Solve, Undo, Reset and the size switcher are in the bottom sheet.
• Paid content: tap the locked 5×5 or 6×6 tile → the "Jelly Pass" sheet → Buy (US$2.99, non-consumable, product tech.zhongbei.jellycube3d.extra_types). Sandbox test: 1) purchase → 5×5/6×6 unlock immediately; 2) delete and reinstall, then use Restore Purchases on the same sheet; 3) cancel a purchase → content stays locked.
• Audio and haptics can be muted in-app.

4) EXTERNAL SERVICES USED FOR CORE FUNCTIONALITY
Only Apple In-App Purchase (StoreKit), for the optional one-time unlock and for restoring purchases. No other external services are used: no data providers, no authentication services, no third-party payment processors, no ads, no analytics, no AI services. Gameplay is fully offline.

5) REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
The app functions identically in every region where it is offered — same features, same content, one global binary. The only regional aspect is storefront availability: the app is not offered on the China mainland storefront (no ICP filing). There are no region-specific features or content.

6) REGULATED INDUSTRY / THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Not applicable. The app is a puzzle game and does not operate in a regulated industry. It contains no protected third-party material — all graphics, sounds, and interactions are generated by our own code at runtime (App content rights declaration: does not use third-party content).
