Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-02

Jelly Cube 3D is an offline puzzle game published by [LEGAL ENTITY] (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what the app does with information.

The short version

  • We do not collect, sell, rent, or share personal information.
  • The game has no account system, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and no developer-operated server.
  • Gameplay is playable offline.
  • If you buy the optional “Extra Cube Types” unlock, the purchase is processed by Apple, Google, or Huawei. We receive only the entitlement status, not payment card details.
  • The unlocked status is stored locally on your device so the content works offline.

Information processed by the app

Gameplay. The app stores the current puzzle state and settings in memory/local app storage on the device. This information is not transmitted to us.

Purchases. The optional non-consumable in-app purchase is processed by the store that distributed the app: Apple App Store / StoreKit, Google Play / Google Play Billing, or Huawei AppGallery / IAP Kit. The store may process purchase history and payment information under its own privacy policy. The app receives a transaction/entitlement result and stores an unlock flag locally. We do not receive your name, email address, payment method, or billing address from the store.

Diagnostics. The release build does not include a third-party crash-reporting or analytics SDK. If the operating system or store provides crash reports to the developer, those reports are controlled by that platform and are not collected by the app itself.

Permissions

  • The game does not request microphone, camera, contacts, location, or advertising identifiers.
  • Haptics use the platform haptic API; no permission is required on iOS.
  • Gameplay does not use the network. The Android build includes Google Play Billing, which declares INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE for communication with the Play Store. The app itself does not send gameplay or analytics data.

Children

The game is a general-audience puzzle and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data retention and deletion

We do not hold a server-side account or personal data. The local unlock flag remains on the device until the app is removed or its data is cleared. Store purchase records are retained by the relevant store under its policies.

Third-party services

  • Apple privacy
  • Google privacy
  • Huawei privacy

No advertising, analytics, or social SDK is included.

Changes

If the app later adds accounts, ads, analytics, cloud sync, or a developer server, this policy and store disclosures will be updated before that release.
