Jelly Cube 3D

Support

Twist a layer

Drag across a cubelet face. The slice follows your finger and locks at 90°.

Rotate / zoom

Drag outside the cube, or select Spin. Two fingers orbit and pinch-to-zoom.

Poke / drop

Select Poke or Drop, then tap the cube. A stylus can use pressure for a deeper dent.

I bought Extra Cube Types but 5×5/6×6 is locked

Open the purchase sheet and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same store account. If it still does not appear, email support with the store, date, and approximate time. Do not send card details. HarmonyOS v1 does not offer Extra Cube Types; it supports 2×2–4×4.
