# Jelly Cube 3D — Support

## Quick help

### How do I twist a layer?
Drag across a cubelet face. The slice follows your finger and locks to a 90° turn when it passes 45°.

### How do I rotate the whole cube?
Drag outside the cube, or select the Spin tool. Two fingers orbit and pinch-to-zoom.

### How do I poke?
Select Poke and tap the cube. A stylus can use pressure for a deeper dent.

### How do I drop the cube?
Select Drop and tap the cube, or press Space on a keyboard.

### I bought Extra Cube Types but 5×5/6×6 is locked
Open the purchase screen and tap **Restore Purchases**. Make sure you are signed into the same store account that made the purchase. If it still does not appear, contact support with the store, date, and approximate time of purchase. Do not send payment card details.

### How do I get a refund?
Refunds are handled by the store:
- Apple: report a problem at reportaproblem.apple.com
- Google Play: Play Store → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history
- Huawei: AppGallery purchase history / Huawei support

### Is the game online?
No. Gameplay is offline. Optional purchases use the platform store.

### Does the game collect data?
No. See the privacy policy linked from the store listing and the app.

### Audio or haptics are not working
Check the mute switch and the in-app mute button. Haptics can be disabled in the app’s settings. On supported devices, haptics respect the system settings.
