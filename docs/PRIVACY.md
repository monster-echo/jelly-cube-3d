# Privacy Policy — Jelly Cube 3D

Last updated: 2026-11-02

Jelly Cube 3D is an offline puzzle game published by **[LEGAL ENTITY]** ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with information.

## The short version

- We do not collect, sell, rent, or share personal information.
- The game has no account system, no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and no developer-operated server.
- The game is playable offline.
- If you buy the optional “Extra Cube Types” unlock, the purchase is processed by Apple, Google, or Huawei. We receive only the entitlement status, not your payment card details.
- The unlocked status is stored locally on your device so the content works offline.

## Information processed by the app

### Gameplay

The app stores your current puzzle state and settings in memory/local app storage on the device. This information is not transmitted to us.

### Purchases

The optional non-consumable in-app purchase is processed by the store that distributed the app:

- Apple App Store / StoreKit
- Google Play / Google Play Billing
- Huawei AppGallery / IAP Kit

The store may process purchase history and payment information under its own privacy policy. The app receives a transaction/entitlement result and stores an unlock flag locally. We do not receive your name, email address, payment method, or billing address from the store.

### Diagnostics

The release build does not include a third-party crash-reporting or analytics SDK. If the operating system or store provides crash reports to the developer, those reports are controlled by that platform and are not collected by the app itself.

## Permissions

- The game does not request microphone, camera, contacts, location, or advertising identifiers.
- Haptics are provided through the platform haptic API. No permission is required on iOS; Android uses the platform haptic feedback API.
- The app does not request network permission for gameplay. Store billing may use the platform store service.

## Children

The game is a general-audience puzzle and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help.

## Data retention and deletion

We do not hold a server-side account or personal data. The local unlock flag remains on the device until the app is removed or its data is cleared. Store purchase records are retained by the relevant store under its policies.

## Third-party services

The app relies on the platform store for optional purchases:

- Apple: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
- Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Huawei: https://consumer.huawei.com/en/privacy/

No advertising, analytics, or social SDK is included.

## Changes

If the app later adds accounts, ads, analytics, cloud sync, or a developer server, this policy and the store privacy disclosures will be updated before that release.
